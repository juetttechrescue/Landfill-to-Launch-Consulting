The part nobody prepares you for

Getting set up is not the hard part. What comes after is.

Landing your website and your first few customers is work, but it is not what stops people. The stretch that stops people is the one after that: you have traction, you are addressing a real gap, but the partnerships and infrastructure that make it compound are not signed yet.

That waiting period is where most people stall. It is also where the money is won or lost, because everything comes down to how you handle the waste you collect. I have been through it, and I help you move through it deliberately instead of by guessing.

Everything you collect goes one of four ways

01

Responsible recycling

Deciding what truly belongs in the recycling stream.

02

Reallocation

Recognizing what is worth routing elsewhere first.

03

Community initiatives

Where doing good and building the brand meet.

04

Liquidation tactics

Moving the right items, at the right moment.

The four channels are obvious. Knowing how to weight them, for your market and for the moment you are in, is not. That balance is the difference between breaking even and compounding, and it is exactly what the Blueprint builds for you.

The case study

Juett Tech Rescue: the model, proven

Landfill to Launch is not theory. It is built on a business I started, run, and grew in a real market. Juett Tech Rescue is the test case that shows the playbook works.

I built and run Juett Tech Rescue, the first electronic-waste management service in Boulder County, Colorado. The business makes e-waste recycling affordable and convenient through a flat-fee, door-to-door model built to remove the barriers that keep people from recycling electronics.

Eco-Cycle, one of the nation's largest and oldest nonprofit recycling facilities, reached out to me directly to explore a partnership. When the top local processor calls you, that is real demand.

I started paying attention to electronic waste when I realized almost no one was targeting it head-on. There is no federal blanket ban on sending electronics to landfill, so the logistics are inconsistent from one state to the next. Even where it is regulated, it often is not addressed directly. And it is the fastest-growing solid waste stream in the world.

That gap is the opportunity. Juett Tech Rescue is how I proved the model in one market. Landfill to Launch is how I help you build it in yours.
